## Description
How did a dented cap of hammered copper become the modern combat helmet? For 5,000 years, every new helmet existed for one reason: the one before it had failed.

In this documentary, we trace the helmet from the copper caps of ancient Sumer to the Kevlar and polyethylene helmets of today. Along the way: the Greek Corinthian helmet that protected almost everything but cut soldiers off from the battlefield, the Roman helmets built for an empire of hundreds of thousands, the medieval great helm and its fatal flat top, the dog-faced visor of the hounskull, the century when soldiers stopped wearing helmets at all, and the First World War shells that brought them back.

You'll see why the US M1 helmet's hidden gap saved lives, how a 1965 lab discovery changed armor forever, and why an American helmet from the 1980s looked almost exactly like a German one from 1916.
