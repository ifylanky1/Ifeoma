# The Evolution of the Helmet: Google Flow Video Prompts (8-second clips)

Google Flow makes 8-second clips, so each of the 30 shots is split into 8-second clips that follow the narration. 81 clips in total.

## How to use in Google Flow
- **Clip 1 of each shot:** use Frames to Video, upload that shot's image as the first frame, and paste the Clip 1 prompt.
- **Clip 2, 3, 4:** add the clip to Scenebuilder and use Extend, which continues from the last frame of the previous clip, then paste the next prompt. If Extend isn't available, use Frames to Video again with the same shot image and the next prompt.
- **Sound:** mute the clips in your editor. Your voiceover replaces the audio.
- **Fitting the voiceover:** some clips cover 4–7 seconds of narration and some about 9. Trim the extra, or slow a clip slightly to cover the gap.
