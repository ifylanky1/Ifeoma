## LAYOUT: pick one mode

**MODE 1: VIDEO BACKGROUND** (the one image for the whole video)
> Centered symmetrical composition with the subject as the single focal point, lower fifth of the frame dark and uncluttered for captions, calm and still enough to hold for an hour.

**MODE 2: THUMBNAIL ART** (text is added afterward in Canva)
> Subject placed on the right 45% of the frame, angled slightly toward the viewer, bursting with light, sparks and energy; the left 55% of the frame fades to near-black smoky darkness and is completely empty, as negative space for text; bottom-right corner clear; high contrast, dramatic, intense.

**MODE 3: THUMBNAIL ALL-IN-ONE** (only for text-capable generators such as Ideogram or GPT image)
> Use Mode 2, then replace "no text, no letters" with: Left side bold condensed all-caps headline "[LINE 1]" in silver-white metallic with dark stroke and "[LINE 2]" in fire-gold gradient with glow, small gold flourish divider, gold condensed subline "[SUBLINE, 6–14 WORDS]", small ribbon "[CHANNEL NAME]" between gold scroll ornaments.

## RULES THAT KEEP THE CHANNEL LOOK CONSISTENT
1. Use ONE focal subject per image. Never crowd the frame.
2. Keep the palette warm: crimson, orange and gold. No blues or greens. Light should feel divine, not hellish.
3. No faces on video backgrounds or thumbnails. Sacred objects perform better and avoid AI face distortion.
4. Always leave space for text: the dark lower fifth for video captions, the dark left 55% for thumbnail text.
5. Embers and smoke appear in every image. They're the channel's visual signature.
6. Keep the "no text" instruction in except in Mode 3, and add text in Canva for clean lettering.
7. Change only the subject, setting, action, mood and layout. Never change the style sentences.
