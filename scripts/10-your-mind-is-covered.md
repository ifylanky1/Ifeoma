[THE NIGHTLY WATCH]

Here is why. A tired, anxious mind often gets louder at night. The day's distractions are gone, and every worry has room to speak. But when you fill that quiet with the Word of God, night after night, your mind begins to learn a new rhythm. Many who pray this way find that falling asleep becomes a little easier, that the heaviness lifts a little sooner, and that peace starts to feel familiar again.

And let me say this clearly, with love. Struggling with your mind is not a sign that you have no faith. Some of the strongest servants of God in the Bible fought fear, discouragement, and despair. And God works through many kinds of help. Through prayer and his Word. Through his people. Through counselors and doctors and medicine. Receive all of it as his care for you. And if you are having thoughts of ending your life tonight, please reach out right now to someone you trust, or to your local emergency number or crisis line. You matter, and you are not alone.

Now let me tell you about a man who reached the end of himself. His name was Elijah. He had just seen fire fall from heaven on Mount Carmel. It was the greatest victory of his life. And then a single threatening message came from Queen Jezebel, and Elijah ran for his life. He ran into the wilderness, alone, and he collapsed under a tree.

It is written in First Kings 19 and verses 4 and 5. But he himself went a day’s journey into the wilderness, and came and sat down under a juniper tree: and he requested for himself that he might die; and said, It is enough; now, O Lord, take away my life; for I am not better than my fathers. And as he lay and slept under a juniper tree, behold, then an angel touched him, and said unto him, Arise and eat.

He told God, I have had enough. Take my life. And what did God do? He did not shout at him. He did not tell him he lacked faith. He let him sleep. Then an angel touched him and gave him bread and water. And Elijah slept again. And then the angel came back. It is written in First Kings 19 and verse 7. And the angel of the Lord came again the second time, and touched him, and said, Arise and eat; because the journey is too great for thee.

The journey is too great for thee. God knew. God understood. He gave Elijah rest, and food, and a gentle touch, and only then did he speak to him about what was next.

That is what tonight is. God is not disappointed in you for being tired. He knows the journey has been too great. And tonight, before anything else, he wants you to rest.

Perfect peace. Not because every problem is solved. Not because you have it all figured out. Perfect peace because your mind is stayed on him. Leaning on him. Resting on him. Trusting in him.
