# Video Prompt: "3AM Warfare Prayer"

Use: image-to-video. Upload the generated master image as the start frame (and the end frame too, if the tool allows it, for a seamless loop). Generate one 8–10 second clip and loop it for the full video.

## MASTER VIDEO PROMPT (for the master image)

**Video Prompt:**
Gentle, almost still cinematic motion inside an ancient stone prison cell at midnight. Warm molten-gold light pours steadily through the open prison doorway, with a slow, soft breathing pulse that brightens and dims over several seconds. Dust and thin smoke drift slowly through the beams of light, catching the glow. The broken chain links on the floor glow faintly ember-orange at their snapped ends, pulsing gently. The faint golden crack in the stone wall shimmers softly. The small oil lamp flame on the ledge flickers and sways. Glowing embers and ash particles float upward through the air at a slow, calm pace. The camera holds almost still with an extremely slow push-in toward the open door. The door does not move. No people, no sudden movement, no shaking, no cuts. Hopeful, sacred, peaceful atmosphere. Painterly devotional art style preserved exactly; crimson, ember orange and molten gold palette; no cool tones.

- **Camera Motion:** Locked off with a very slow push-in of about 2 percent over the clip
- **Motion Elements:** A pulsing glow through the doorway, drifting dust and smoke in the light beams, faintly glowing chain ends, a shimmering crack, lamp flicker, rising embers
- **Speed:** Slow and calm, suitable for sleep listening
- **Duration:** 8–10 seconds, looped

**Negative Prompt:**
earthquake, shaking, falling rocks, door swinging, chains moving, fast motion, camera shake, zoom bursts, people, faces, hands, silhouettes in the doorway, morphing objects, melting shapes, flicker artifacts, strobing, color shift, blue tones, text, logos, watermark, low quality, distortion

**Settings (most image-to-video tools):**
- Aspect ratio 16:9, 1080p or higher
- Motion strength: low (about 2–3 out of 10)
- Creativity or prompt strength: low to keep the painting unchanged
- Use the same image as the start and end frame for a seamless loop

## VIDEO PROMPT: ALTERNATE A (the clock at three)

Gentle, almost still motion in a dark stone hallway. The clock's pendulum swings very slowly and evenly, catching the amber light. The clock hands stay still. The glowing cross pulses softly with white-gold light, and the dark smoke at the frame edges curls slowly but never advances. The candle flame flickers and sways. Embers float upward. Camera locked with a very slow push-in. Watchful, protective, calm mood; painterly style and crimson-gold palette preserved; no cool tones.

Negative prompt: clock hands moving, fast pendulum, fast motion, camera shake, people, shadows of figures, text, numbers, logos, morphing, blue tones.

## VIDEO PROMPT: ALTERNATE B (chariots of fire around the house)

Slow cinematic motion over a house on a hill before dawn. The horses and chariots of fire stand in place around the hills while their flames flicker and ripple gently. The horses stay still apart from a soft shimmer of fire in their manes. The house windows glow with a steady warmth. The band of crimson and gold light on the horizon brightens very slowly. Clouds drift slowly overhead. Embers rise. Camera almost still with a very slow push-in toward the house. Majestic, protective, peaceful mood; painterly style and crimson-gold palette preserved; no cool tones.

Negative prompt: galloping, charging, horses moving legs, fast motion, camera shake, people, riders' faces, text, logos, morphing, blue tones, flicker artifacts.

## LOOP AND EDIT ASSEMBLY

1. Generate the 8–10 second clip from the master image and check that the first and last frames match.
2. If the loop shows a jump, add a 1-second crossfade at the loop point, or play the clip forward and then reversed.
3. Repeat the clip to cover the full voiceover (about an hour).
4. Group all the copies into one compound clip (CapCut) or nested sequence (Premiere), then add one slow zoom of 5–8 percent across the whole thing.
5. Add the ember and ash overlay and the smoke overlay at low opacity.
6. Burn in the captions: bold white sans-serif, soft shadow, centered at the bottom, 1 to 2 lines.
