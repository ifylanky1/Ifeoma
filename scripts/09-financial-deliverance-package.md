## E. Description

Does your money keep disappearing no matter how hard you work? Tonight, bring your little oil to God.

Lie down, press play, and let the Word of God break every chain of lack over your life, your home and your children.

In this prayer:

🛡️ The bag with holes mended, and the devourer rebuked over your work and finances

🔥 The poverty curse and generational patterns of lack broken by the Blood of Jesus

🩸 Freedom from debt, closed doors of work opened, and everything stolen recovered

📖 19 scriptures declared over your provision, word for word from the King James Bible

"But thou shalt remember the LORD thy God: for it is he that giveth thee power to get wealth." Deuteronomy 8:18

Play this nightly before you sleep. Let it run while you rest for 7 nights, then 14, then 30. And when you wake, ask the widow's question: what do I have in the house?

Type "My God shall supply all my need." in the comments as your declaration of faith.

➡️ Subscribe to Shielded Tonight Prayers and pray with us every night. You are covered, you are seen, and this family of faith is praying beside you.
