## PART 1 of 7: Hook to The Court Is in Session (1256 words)

Tonight, before you sleep, you are going to court. Not a court on earth, where the powerful win and the poor wait for years. The court of heaven. And you are not walking in there alone. You are not walking in guilty. You are not walking in without a defender. Tonight you walk in covered by the blood of Jesus Christ, with the greatest Advocate who ever stood before a throne.

Some of what you have been fighting did not start with you. It was signed before you were born. Agreements made in your family line. Words spoken over your life. Doors opened by choices you never made, and some you did. And the enemy has been holding those papers up, again and again, saying, I have a right to this life. I have a right to this home. I have a right to this marriage, these children, this money, this destiny. And for years, you have felt it. The same struggles. The same losses. The same closed doors, no matter how hard you pray.

Tonight those papers go before the Judge. And the Judge has already seen the cross.

It is written in Colossians 2 and verse 14. Blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us, which was contrary to us, and took it out of the way, nailing it to his cross.

The same God who silenced the accuser standing beside Joshua the high priest. The same God who heard the widow who would not stop knocking. The same God who set the judgment and opened the books. That God is seated on the throne tonight. So hear me, every power holding a claim against this life. Bring your papers. Bring your charges. You are about to find out they have already been nailed to a cross.

Before we step into that courtroom, I want to ask you for something. Make this prayer part of your nights. Play it every night before you sleep. Let it run while you rest. Seven nights. Then fourteen. Then thirty.

Here is why. A court case is not settled by showing up once and walking out early. You come back. You stand on the same promise again. You keep your case before the Judge until the verdict is enforced in every corner of your life. Many of the battles you face are old. They have deep roots. And deep roots are pulled out with steady hands, night after night.

Many who pray this way, night after night, begin to notice change. Cycles that ran for years begin to loosen. Strange patterns in the family begin to stop. Peace begins to settle where there was always tension. Not because a voice on a speaker has power, but because the Word of God does what it says, and you are giving it room to work.

Let me take you into a courtroom from the Bible. The prophet Zechariah was shown a vision. He saw Joshua, the high priest of Israel, standing before the angel of the Lord. And Joshua was not alone. It is written in Zechariah 3 and verses 1 and 2. And he shewed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of the LORD, and Satan standing at his right hand to resist him. And the LORD said unto Satan, The LORD rebuke thee, O Satan; even the LORD that hath chosen Jerusalem rebuke thee: is not this a brand plucked out of the fire?

Picture that scene. Joshua is standing there in filthy clothes. The accuser is standing right beside him, ready to argue against him. And honestly, the accuser has material. Joshua's garments are dirty. His people have a long history of failure. If the case were judged on Joshua's record alone, he would lose.

But Joshua does not defend himself. He does not say one word. The Lord speaks for him. The Lord rebukes the accuser. And then the Lord says something beautiful. Is not this a brand plucked out of the fire? In other words, I pulled this one out of the fire myself. You do not get him back.

Then the filthy garments are taken away, and Joshua is clothed in clean robes. The accusation is not argued down. It is overruled.

That is your story tonight. You may feel like you have been standing in dirty clothes, with an accuser at your side who knows every failure. But you are a brand plucked out of the fire. The Lord who chose you is the Lord who rebukes your accuser. So keep coming into this courtroom, night after night. You are not here to be condemned. You are here to be cleared.

Now listen closely, because there is one thing the enemy cannot answer in the court of heaven. He has his charges. He has his records. He has his long list of everything you and your family have ever done wrong. And many times, he is not lying about the facts. That is what makes his accusations hurt. He knows the night you said the words you regret. He knows the door your grandfather opened. He knows the sin you thought was buried. He brings it all into court, and he reads it out slowly, hoping you will hang your head and agree with him.

But there is one piece of evidence he cannot overcome. It is not your good works. It is not your promises to do better. It is the blood of Jesus Christ, already presented before the throne, already accepted by the Judge.

The enemy wants you to believe the courtroom of heaven is a place to fear. It is not. For the believer, it is a throne of grace. It is written in Hebrews 4 and verse 16. Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need.

Boldly. Not crawling. Not hiding. Not apologizing for being there. You come boldly because of who is speaking for you. You are not a stranger in that courtroom. You are a child of the Judge. And no father sits on a throne and hands his own child over to the one who hates them.

And here is what the Judge did with the papers against you. It is written again in Colossians 2 and verse 14. Blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us, which was contrary to us, and took it out of the way, nailing it to his cross.

Blotted out. The ink is gone. Taken out of the way. It is no longer in the file. Nailed to his cross. Paid in full and put on public display, so that every power of darkness can see the debt is settled.

So when the enemy brings up an old agreement, an old sin, an old covenant from your bloodline, you do not argue the facts. You point to the cross. That record was blotted out. That claim was taken out of the way. That debt was nailed to the tree.

So say this with me. Say it out loud, right where you are lying. I come boldly to the throne of grace. I do not come in my own name. I come in the name of Jesus Christ. Every handwriting against me is blotted out. Every claim against me is taken out of the way. Every debt against me is nailed to the cross. The blood of Jesus speaks for me tonight, and the Judge has heard it.

I bring them all before the Judge tonight.

You remind me of my past, but I have been given a new future. Your record is old. His mercy is new every morning.

I hold this word up before the throne like a court order. The accuser is cast down. Not standing. Not winning. Cast down. And it is written in Romans 8 and verses 33 and 34. Who shall lay any thing to the charge of God's elect? It is God that justifieth. Who is he that condemneth? It is Christ that died, yea rather, that is risen again, who is even at the right hand of God, who also maketh intercession for us.

Who can bring a charge? No one, because God has declared me righteous. Who can condemn? No one, because Christ died for me, rose for me, and is praying for me right now.

Accusation, be silenced. Charge, be dismissed. Accuser, be cast down. Old record, be answered by the blood. Condemning voice in my mind, be still. Intercession of Christ, cover me.

The accuser is cast down. My Advocate is risen. I stand justified tonight.

I bring every written record of darkness against my life before the throne, and I declare it blotted out by the blood of Jesus. Every document signed in the spirit against my name. Every agreement drawn up in darkness concerning my destiny. Every register where my name was entered for evil. Every decree issued to limit my life. Every written sentence of failure, sickness, delay, or early death. Tonight, the Judge reviews the file.

And here is what the file says. Blotted out. Taken out of the way. Nailed to the cross. Every ordinance against me has lost its ink. Every page that held my name in darkness is wiped clean. Every seal on those documents is broken by the higher seal of the blood of the Lamb. Whatever power wrote against me did not hold the final pen. God holds the final pen. And what he writes over my life tonight is freedom.

It is written in Colossians 2 and verse 14. Blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us, which was contrary to us, and took it out of the way, nailing it to his cross.

I nail this word to the door of every dark archive. What was written against me is erased. What was contrary to me is removed. What was held over me is nailed to the cross, where everyone can see it was paid.

Every dark register with my name in it, be burned by the fire of God. Every decree of darkness against my life, be overturned in the court of heaven. Every legal paper of the enemy, be declared void.

Handwriting, be blotted out. Ordinance, be taken away. Evil register, burn. Dark decree, be overturned. Seal of darkness, break. Seal of the blood, stand.

The handwriting is gone. The file is clean. Nothing written in darkness can stand against me.
