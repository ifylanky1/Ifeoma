## E. Description

Waking up shaking from the same bad dreams? Tonight, the gates of your dreams are sealed.

Lie down, press play, and let the Word of God cover your sleep, your mind and your imagination.

In this prayer:

🛡️ Dreams of being chased, attacked, pressed down and taken backward cancelled

🔥 Evil food in dreams, unclean night visitors and evil seeds rejected by the Blood of Jesus

🩸 Your children's night terrors silenced and your mind guarded by the peace of God

📖 20 scriptures declared over your sleep, word for word from the King James Bible

"When thou liest down, thou shalt not be afraid: yea, thou shalt lie down, and thy sleep shall be sweet." Proverbs 3:24

If your nightmares come from trauma, or your sleep is troubled by a health problem, please also speak to a counselor or doctor. God works through them too.

Play this nightly before you sleep for 7 nights, then 14, then 30.

Type "My dreams belong to God." in the comments as your declaration of faith.

➡️ Subscribe to Shielded Tonight Prayers and pray with us every night. You are covered, you are seen, and this family of faith is praying beside you.
