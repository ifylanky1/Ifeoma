# Thumbnails: "The Blood of Jesus Over Your Home"

How to build each one: generate the background art from the prompt (the left side is left dark and empty), then add the text in Canva or Photoshop using the shared text spec. If you use a generator that renders text well (Ideogram, GPT image), use the "All-in-one" line instead.

The ribbon carries the channel name, SHIELDED TONIGHT PRAYERS.

## Shared text spec (all 5)
- **Headline font:** Anton or Oswald Bold (condensed sans), or Playfair Display SC Black for the serif variant. All caps, tight leading.
- **Headline line 1:** silver-white metallic with a subtle bevel, a 6–8 px dark stroke and a soft black outer glow.
- **Headline payoff line:** fire-gold gradient (#FFE7A0 at the top, then #F7B733, then #E2571E at the bottom) with a slight inner lava texture and the same stroke and glow.
- **Divider:** a small gold flourish with a center diamond between the headline and the subline.
- **Subline:** Oswald SemiBold in gold #F5C542 with a 4 px dark stroke, 3 lines, about 38% of the headline size.
- **Ribbon (optional):** channel name in spaced white small caps between gold scroll flourishes, under the subline.
- **Layout:** the text block fills the left 55–60% of the frame. Keep the bottom-right 15% clear for the timestamp. Canvas is 1280×720.
