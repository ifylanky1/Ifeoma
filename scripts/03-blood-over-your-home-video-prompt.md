# Video Prompt: "The Blood of Jesus Over Your Home"

Use: image-to-video. Upload the generated master image as the start frame (and the end frame too, if the tool allows it, for a seamless loop). Generate one 8–10 second clip and loop it for the full video.

## MASTER VIDEO PROMPT (for the master image)

**Video Prompt:**
Gentle, almost still cinematic motion on a sacred night scene. The candle flame on the stone doorstep flickers softly and sways, its warm light pulsing gently across the wood of the dark oak door. The crimson marks on the doorposts and lintel glow with a slow, steady breathing pulse of molten gold light, brightening and dimming over several seconds. The cross shape in the door glows faintly brighter in rhythm with the pulse. Thin smoke drifts slowly from left to right across the threshold and curls upward. Glowing embers and ash particles float upward and sideways through the air at a slow, calm pace. The scarlet cord in the window sways very slightly in a soft breeze. Warm golden light shimmers through the gap beneath the door. The camera holds almost still with an extremely slow push-in toward the door. No people, no sudden movement, no cuts. Reverent, peaceful, protective atmosphere. Painterly devotional art style preserved exactly; crimson, ember orange and molten gold palette; no cool tones.

- **Camera Motion:** Locked off with a very slow push-in of about 2 percent over the clip
- **Motion Elements:** Candle flicker, a pulsing glow on the doorposts, drifting smoke, rising embers, a slight sway of the cord, shimmering light under the door
- **Speed:** Slow and calm, suitable for sleep listening
- **Duration:** 8–10 seconds, looped

**Negative Prompt:**
fast motion, camera shake, zoom bursts, door opening, people, faces, hands, morphing objects, melting shapes, flicker artifacts, strobing, color shift, blue tones, text, logos, watermark, low quality, distortion

**Settings (most image-to-video tools):**
- Aspect ratio 16:9, 1080p or higher
- Motion strength: low (about 2–3 out of 10)
- Creativity or prompt strength: low to keep the painting unchanged
- Use the same image as the start and end frame for a seamless loop

## VIDEO PROMPT: ALTERNATE A (house under the covering)

Slow cinematic motion over a family house on a hill at night. The low ring of fire around the property flickers and dances gently. Golden god rays from the cross in the sky shimmer and slowly sweep over the roof. Crimson storm clouds roll very slowly at the edges of the frame while the opening around the cross stays still. The house windows glow with a soft, steady warmth. Embers and ash rise slowly through the air. Dark smoke at the frame edges drifts but never crosses the ring of fire. Camera almost still with a very slow push-in toward the house. Calm, protective, majestic mood; painterly devotional style and crimson-gold palette preserved; no cool tones.

Negative prompt: fast motion, lightning strikes, camera shake, people, text, logos, morphing, blue tones, flicker artifacts.

## VIDEO PROMPT: ALTERNATE B (the guarded door)

Gentle, almost still motion. The lantern held by Jesus glows with a soft flicker. His robe and hair move very slightly in a light breeze, and his face and pose stay calm and still. The golden halo behind his head pulses slowly. The crimson doorposts behind him glow softly. The angel in the background stays still while its flaming sword flickers gently and its wings shimmer with light. Smoke drifts slowly across the scene and embers float upward. Camera locked with a very slow push-in. Reverent, protective, majestic mood; painterly oil-painting style and crimson-gold palette preserved; no cool tones.

Negative prompt: walking, talking, facial changes, lip movement, fast motion, morphing faces, extra limbs, camera shake, text, logos, blue tones.

## LOOP AND EDIT ASSEMBLY

1. Generate the 8–10 second clip from the master image and check that the first and last frames match.
2. If the loop shows a jump, add a 1-second crossfade at the loop point, or play the clip forward and then reversed.
3. Repeat the clip to cover the full voiceover (about 48 minutes for one cycle, about 2.5 hours for three).
4. Add a slow zoom of 5–8 percent across each prayer cycle in the editor, on top of the clip's own motion.
5. Add the ember and ash overlay and the smoke overlay at low opacity.
6. Burn in the captions: bold white sans-serif, soft shadow, centered at the bottom, 1 to 2 lines.
