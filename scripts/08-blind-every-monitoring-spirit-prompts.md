# Image, Video and Thumbnail Prompts: "Blind Every Monitoring Spirit"

## IMAGE PROMPT: MASTER (use this one)

**Image Prompt:**
A small stone house with warm glowing windows on a dark hillside at night, wrapped in a vast, soft canopy of two enormous wings made of golden light that curve down over the roof and walls like the shadow of the Almighty. Beyond the wings, in the dark sky and surrounding hills, faint glowing red points like watching eyes hang in the smoky darkness, but each one is dimming, clouding over and going dark where the golden light reaches it. A soft pillar of luminous cloud stands between the house and the darkness. Glowing motes and embers drift upward. Cinematic devotional digital painting, painterly realism, sacred Christian concept art. Palette of deep crimson, burnt orange, ember red and smoky maroon with molten gold and warm amber highlights and near-black brown shadows, no cool tones. A single dominant warm light source from the wings and the house, volumetric light, strong chiaroscuro, heavy dark vignette. Highly detailed textures on the stone and the feathered light; soft misty painterly background. Centered composition with the house under the wings as the single focal subject; lower fifth of the frame is dark hillside, kept uncluttered for captions. Wide shot, slightly low angle. Protective, hidden, peaceful mood. 16:9, 4K, no text, no logos, no watermark, no people, no faces.

- **Camera Angle:** Wide, slightly low, centered

## VIDEO PROMPT: MASTER (for the master image)

**Video Prompt:**
Gentle, almost still cinematic motion over a house on a hill at night. The great wings of golden light over the house shimmer softly, with a slow breathing pulse of light running along their feathers. The windows glow steadily. In the dark sky and hills beyond, the faint red points of watching eyes slowly dim and fade into darkness one by one as the golden light reaches them. The pillar of luminous cloud glows softly. Smoke drifts slowly at the edges. Golden motes and embers rise at a calm pace. The camera holds almost still with an extremely slow push-in toward the house. No people, no faces, no sudden movement, no cuts. Protective, peaceful atmosphere. Painterly devotional art style preserved exactly; crimson, ember orange and molten gold palette; no cool tones.

- **Camera Motion:** Locked off with a very slow push-in of about 2 percent
- **Motion Elements:** Shimmering wings with a slow pulse, steady windows, red eye-points slowly fading out, glowing cloud, drifting smoke, rising motes
- **Speed:** Slow and calm. **Duration:** 8–10 seconds, looped

**Negative Prompt:** eyes opening, faces, creatures, people, fast motion, flashing, camera shake, wings flapping, morphing objects, flicker artifacts, strobing, color shift, blue tones, text, logos, watermark, distortion

**Settings:** 16:9, 1080p or higher; motion strength about 2–3 out of 10; low creativity; the same image as the start and end frame. Note: for a seamless loop, the eye-points should be faint at both the start and the end of the clip.
