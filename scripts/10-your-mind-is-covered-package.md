# Package Plan: "Your Mind Is Covered"

## Inputs used
- **Channel name:** Shielded Tonight Prayers
- **Topic:** Your Mind Is Covered | Prayer Against Fear, Anxiety and Spiritual Heaviness Tonight (video idea #10)
- **Length:** 4,916 spoken words, about 35 minutes at 140 words per minute. Played once.
- **Chorus scripture:** Isaiah 26:3
- **Bible story for the testimony:** Elijah under the juniper tree, given sleep, food and a gentle touch, then the still small voice (1 Kings 19)
- **Tone:** gentler than the warfare videos. It still declares and prays with authority, but it treats fear, anxiety, depression, grief and exhaustion with tenderness.
- **Integrity and safety:** the prayer says plainly that struggling with your mind is not a lack of faith, that God works through counselors, doctors and medicine, and that anyone having thoughts of ending their life should reach out immediately to someone they trust or to their local emergency number or crisis line. It does not call mental illness demonic and makes no promise of healing.

## E. Description

Racing thoughts, heavy heart, worry that will not switch off? Tonight, your mind is covered.

Lie down, press play, and let the gentle voice of God settle over your thoughts, your memories and your sleep.

In this prayer:

🕊️ Fear, anxiety about tomorrow and racing thoughts handed to God

🌿 Heaviness, exhaustion, loneliness and grief met with the tenderness of Jesus

🩸 Your mind and your household covered by the Blood of Jesus and the peace of God

📖 20 scriptures for a weary mind, word for word from the King James Bible

"Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee." Isaiah 26:3

Struggling with your mind is not a lack of faith. God works through prayer and through counselors, doctors and medicine. If you are having thoughts of ending your life, please reach out right now to someone you trust, or to your local emergency number or crisis line. You matter.

Play this nightly before you sleep for 7 nights, then 14, then 30.

Type "My mind is covered by the peace of God." in the comments as your declaration of faith.

➡️ Subscribe to Shielded Tonight Prayers and pray with us every night. You are covered, you are seen, and this family of faith is praying beside you.
