## E. Description

Does every good thing in your life get attacked just as it begins? Tonight, you go into the secret place of the Most High.

Lie down, press play, and let the Word of God hide your life, your plans and your family from every eye of darkness.

In this prayer:

🛡️ Every evil eye closed and every monitoring spirit blinded, as at Elisha's prayer

🔥 Every report of your movements burned, and every plan made against you scattered

🩸 Your home, marriage, children and work hidden in the pavilion of the Lord

📖 18 scriptures declared over your life, word for word from the King James Bible

"He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty." Psalm 91:1

This is not a prayer to make you suspicious of the people around you. Our battle is not against flesh and blood.

Play this nightly before you sleep for 7 nights, then 14, then 30.

Type "My life is hidden with Christ in God." in the comments as your declaration of faith.

➡️ Subscribe to Shielded Tonight Prayers and pray with us every night. You are covered, you are seen, and this family of faith is praying beside you.
