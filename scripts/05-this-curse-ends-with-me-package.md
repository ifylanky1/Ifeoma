## E. Description

The same struggle in your grandmother's life, your mother's life, and now yours? Tonight, it stops.

Lie down, press play, and let the Word of God break every generational pattern over your life, your marriage and your children.

In this prayer:

🛡️ Family patterns of fear, temper, addiction and broken marriages exposed and broken

🔥 Generational curses broken by the Blood of Jesus, and family sins brought to the cross

🩸 Your children and your children's children covered with mercy to a thousand generations

📖 19 scriptures declared over your family line, word for word from the King James Bible

"Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us." Galatians 3:13

Play this nightly before you sleep for 7 nights, then 14, then 30.

Type "This curse ends with me." in the comments as your declaration of faith.
