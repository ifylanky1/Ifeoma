# Image, Video and Thumbnail Prompts: "Your Mind Is Covered"

This video is gentler than the warfare prayers, so the art keeps the channel's warm palette but swaps fire for soft candlelight and still water.

Beside it, a still pool of water reflects a soft golden glow from a gentle break in the deep crimson-and-amber clouds above. The desert hills fade softly into the warm darkness. Highly detailed textures on the bark, bread, clay and woven blanket; soft misty painterly background. Centered composition with the tree, bread and water as the single focal subject; lower fifth of the frame is dark soft ground and still water, kept uncluttered for captions. Eye-level camera. Tender, restful, safe mood. 16:9, 4K, no text, no logos, no watermark, no people, no hands.

## VIDEO PROMPT: MASTER (for the master image)

**Video Prompt:**
Very gentle, almost still cinematic motion at a desert oasis at night. The small oil lamp flame sways softly. The still pool reflects a slow, soft shimmer of golden light. A few leaves on the juniper branches stir very slightly in a faint warm breeze. The glow in the clouds pulses softly and slowly. Tiny golden motes drift upward like sleepy fireflies. The camera holds almost still with an extremely slow push-in toward the bread and water beneath the tree. The bread, jar and blanket do not move. No people, no hands, no sudden movement, no cuts. Tender, restful, safe atmosphere for falling asleep. Painterly devotional art style preserved exactly; warm amber, honey gold and soft crimson palette; no cool tones.

- **Camera Motion:** Locked off with a very slow push-in of about 1–2 percent
- **Motion Elements:** Swaying lamp flame, soft shimmer on the pool, slightly stirring leaves, a slow glow in the clouds, rising motes
- **Speed:** Very slow and calm. **Duration:** 8–10 seconds, looped

**Negative Prompt:** wind, ripples, splashing, fast motion, camera shake, people, figures, hands, angels with faces, fire, sparks, morphing objects, flicker artifacts, strobing, color shift, blue tones, text, logos, watermark, distortion

**Settings:** 16:9, 1080p or higher; motion strength about 1–2 out of 10; low creativity; the same image as the start and end frame.
