# Video Prompt: "Courtroom of Heaven Prayer"

Use: image-to-video. Upload the generated master image as the start frame (and the end frame too, if the tool allows it, for a seamless loop). Generate one 8–10 second clip and loop it for the full video.

## MASTER VIDEO PROMPT (for the master image)

**Video Prompt:**
Gentle, almost still cinematic motion on a sacred night scene. The thin lines of ember-orange fire along the edges of the parchment scroll flicker and glow softly, and tiny flakes of glowing ash lift slowly off the page and drift upward. The clean parts of the parchment glow with a slow, steady breathing pulse of white-gold light. The great shaft of golden light from above shimmers gently, with god rays slowly shifting through the drifting smoke. The crimson storm clouds behind the cross roll very slowly at the edges of the frame. Glowing embers and ash particles float upward through the air at a slow, calm pace. The camera holds almost still with an extremely slow push-in toward the cross. The cross, nail and scroll stay perfectly still in shape. No people, no sudden movement, no cuts. Solemn, triumphant, peaceful atmosphere. Painterly devotional art style preserved exactly; crimson, ember orange and molten gold palette; no cool tones.

- **Camera Motion:** Locked off with a very slow push-in of about 2 percent over the clip
- **Motion Elements:** Flickering fire along the scroll edges, ash lifting off the page, a pulsing glow on the clean parchment, shimmering god rays, slowly rolling clouds, rising embers
- **Speed:** Slow and calm, suitable for sleep listening
- **Duration:** 8–10 seconds, looped

**Negative Prompt:**
scroll burning up completely, paper falling, readable text, letters appearing, fast motion, lightning flashes, camera shake, zoom bursts, people, faces, hands, morphing objects, melting shapes, cross bending, flicker artifacts, strobing, color shift, blue tones, logos, watermark, low quality, distortion

**Settings (most image-to-video tools):**
- Aspect ratio 16:9, 1080p or higher
- Motion strength: low (about 2–3 out of 10)
- Creativity or prompt strength: low to keep the painting unchanged
- Use the same image as the start and end frame for a seamless loop

## VIDEO PROMPT: ALTERNATE A (the throne and the open books)

Slow, majestic motion in a heavenly courtroom. The river of liquid fire flows steadily and slowly down from beneath the blazing throne. The throne's white-gold light pulses gently. The pages of the open books on the lectern glow softly, and one page lifts very slightly in a gentle breeze and settles back. The rows of distant golden lights twinkle faintly. Crimson clouds drift slowly at the edges. Embers rise slowly. Camera almost still with a very slow push-in toward the throne. Holy, majestic, peaceful mood; painterly style and crimson-gold palette preserved; no cool tones.

Negative prompt: figures appearing, faces, pages flipping fast, writing appearing, fast motion, camera shake, explosions, text, logos, morphing, blue tones, flicker artifacts.

## VIDEO PROMPT: ALTERNATE B (the clean robe)

Gentle, almost still motion in a candlelit stone room. The white robe glows with a soft, slow pulse of light, and its hem sways very slightly as if in a faint breeze. The smoldering edges of the dark garments on the floor glow and fade gently, giving off thin wisps of smoke. The candle flame flickers and sways. Dust drifts slowly through the shaft of golden light. Embers float upward. Camera locked with a very slow push-in toward the robe. Tender, cleansing, hopeful mood; painterly style and crimson-gold palette preserved.

Negative prompt: robe moving off the stand, fabric morphing, fire spreading, fast motion, camera shake, hands, people, text, logos, blue tones.

## LOOP AND EDIT ASSEMBLY

1. Generate the 8–10 second clip from the master image and check that the first and last frames match.
2. If the loop shows a jump, add a 1-second crossfade at the loop point, or play the clip forward and then reversed.
3. Repeat the clip to cover the full voiceover (about an hour).
4. Group all the copies into one compound clip, then add one slow zoom of 5–8 percent across the whole thing.
5. Add the ember and ash overlay and the smoke overlay at low opacity.
6. Burn in the captions: bold white sans-serif, soft shadow, centered at the bottom, 1 to 2 lines.
7. Export from the **CapCut desktop app**, not CapCut in the browser, so the export runs on your laptop.
