## PART 1 of 7: Hook to The Shield of Faith (1147 words)

The same God who turned Haman's gallows on the man who built it. The same God who turned Balaam's curse into a blessing. The same God who let the giant's own sword finish the giant in the hand of a shepherd boy. That God is standing in front of you tonight. He has not moved. He has not slept. And he is not finished.

So hear me, every power of darkness that has been sending arrows into this life. Your season of shooting is over. What you formed in the dark is coming home to you tonight. And this time, it is on fire.

Here is why. A shield is not raised once and then dropped. A soldier on the wall holds it up through the whole watch. The enemy does not stop because you prayed one loud night. He tests the wall again. So we answer him again. Night after night, the shield stays up. Night after night, every arrow meets it. And night after night, the enemy learns that this life is not an open field.

Many who pray this way, night after night, begin to notice something shift. Sleep starts to come more easily. The heaviness in the room begins to lift. Old cycles start to lose their grip. Not by our strength, but because the Word of God does what it says.

Let me tell you about a man who learned how God handles an arrow. His name was Mordecai. He sat at the king's gate in Persia, a faithful man who would not bow where he should not bow. And a powerful man named Haman hated him for it. Haman did not only want Mordecai gone. He wanted Mordecai's whole people destroyed. He cast lots to choose the day. He offered the king silver to make it law. And at his own house, he built a gallows fifty cubits high, and he planned to hang Mordecai on it.

But that same night, the king could not sleep. Did you hear that? On the night the trap was built, heaven kept a king awake. He called for the records to be read to him, and there it was. Mordecai had once saved the king's life, and he had never been honored. By the next day, the man who came to destroy Mordecai was forced to lead him through the city in royal robes.

And then the trap closed on the one who set it. Queen Esther stood before the king and exposed the plot. And it is written in Esther 7 and verse 10. So they hanged Haman on the gallows that he had prepared for Mordecai. Then was the king's wrath pacified.

The gallows was real. The plan was real. The date was set. But none of it touched Mordecai. It went back to the one who built it.

That is what we are praying tonight. Not that we would harm anyone, but that every trap, every arrow, every scheme of darkness would fail against you and fall back on the power that formed it. So keep this shield up, night after night. Because the God who kept a king awake for Mordecai is keeping watch over you.

Now listen closely, because there is one weapon the enemy cannot stand against. He has studied your weaknesses. He knows your history. He knows the hours when you are most tired. But there is one thing he has never been able to break, and it is not your strength. It is the Word of the living God, spoken by a believer covered in the blood of the Lamb.

God put it into one sentence that has outlasted every enemy who ever rose against his people. It is written in Isaiah 54 and verse 17. This is the heritage of the servants of the LORD, and their righteousness is of me, saith the LORD.

Not one will reach its goal. And every tongue that rises against you, you are given the authority to condemn. That is your inheritance. Not a reward for being perfect. A heritage, given to the servants of the Lord.

Then the apostle Paul tells us how we carry it. It is written in Ephesians 6 and verse 16. Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked. All of them. Not some. Not most. All.

For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places. People, we bless. People, we pray for. But the spirits behind the arrows, the powers that drive them, the darkness that forms them, those we confront tonight. And to them, every arrow returns.

So say this with me. Say it out loud, right where you are lying. Let your own ears hear your own voice. No weapon formed against me shall prosper. I raise the shield of faith over my life tonight.
