# Thumbnail Refresh: short-text versions for the first 4 videos

Why: YouTube Analytics showed only 38 impressions and a 2.6% click-through rate. The niche leaders use 2–5 huge words that read on a phone; our first thumbnails had a headline plus a 3-line subline, which is too small to read on mobile. These versions keep the same background art and replace all the text with 2 short lines.

## How to swap a thumbnail on a video that is already live

1. Open **YouTube Studio → Content** and click the video.
2. Under **Thumbnail**, click the three dots on the current one and choose **Change**, then upload the new image. Or use **Test & Compare** to upload the old and new versions and let YouTube pick the winner.
3. Click **Save**. The title, views and comments stay exactly as they are.

## Text spec (all 4)
- **Words:** only the 2 lines below. Delete the subline and the flourish. The small SHIELDED TONIGHT PRAYERS ribbon is optional.
- **Size:** the two lines together fill the left half of the frame. Shrink the finished image to phone size and check you can still read it instantly.
- **Font:** Anton or Oswald Bold, all caps, tight leading.
- **Line 1:** silver-white metallic, 6–8 px dark stroke, soft black outer glow.
- **Line 2:** fire-gold gradient (#FFE7A0 at the top, then #F7B733, then #E2571E at the bottom), same stroke and glow.
- **Canvas:** 1280×720. Text on the left 55%, the art on the right 45%, bottom-right corner clear for the timestamp.
