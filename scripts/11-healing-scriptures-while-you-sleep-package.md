## E. Description

Lie down, close your eyes, and let the Word of God be the last voice you hear tonight.

One hour of God's promises for healing, peace and protection, read slowly from the King James Bible so you can fall asleep resting in his Word.

In this reading:

🩹 Healing promises: Exodus 15:26, Psalm 103, Isaiah 53, Jeremiah 17:14

🕊️ Peace for an anxious mind: Isaiah 26:3, Philippians 4:6–7, John 14:27

🛡️ Protection through the night: Psalm 91, Psalm 121, Proverbs 3:24

📖 Strength, comfort and God's love: Psalm 23, Isaiah 40, Romans 8

"He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds." Psalm 147:3

This is a reading of Scripture for faith, comfort and rest. It is not medical advice, so please keep following the care of your doctor. God works through medicine and through prayer.

Play this every night before you sleep, and let God's Word fill your room while you rest.

➡️ Subscribe to Shielded Tonight Prayers for nightly prayers and scripture readings. You are covered, you are seen, and this family of faith is praying beside you.
