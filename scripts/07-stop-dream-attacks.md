[HOOK]

[THE NIGHTLY WATCH]

Before we go any further, I want to ask you for something. Make this prayer part of your nights. Play it every night before you sleep, and let it keep playing as you drift off. Seven nights. Then fourteen. Then thirty.

Here is why. What fills your mind as you fall asleep often follows you into your dreams. When the last thing you hear is fear, the night is often full of fear. When the last thing you hear is the Word of God, your mind rests on something stronger. Many who make this a nightly habit begin to notice that their dreams grow quieter, their sleep grows deeper, and they wake up with more peace.

Let me tell you about a man who fell asleep in a dangerous place. His name was Jacob. He was running from his brother, who had sworn to kill him. He was alone in the wilderness, far from home, with nothing but the clothes on his back. When the sun went down, he took a stone for a pillow and lay down on the ground.

If anyone had reason for nightmares, it was Jacob. Guilt. Fear. Loneliness. A brother who wanted him dead. But that night, God gave him a different kind of dream. It is written in Genesis 28 and verse 12. And he dreamed, and behold a ladder set up on the earth, and the top of it reached to heaven: and behold the angels of God ascending and descending on it.

A ladder from earth to heaven, and the angels of God going up and down on it, right over the place where Jacob slept. And above it stood the Lord, who spoke to him. It is written in Genesis 28 and verses 15 and 16. And, behold, I am with thee, and will keep thee in all places whither thou goest, and will bring thee again into this land; for I will not leave thee, until I have done that which I have spoken to thee of. And Jacob awaked out of his sleep, and he said, Surely the Lord is in this place; and I knew it not.

Surely the Lord is in this place, and I knew it not. Jacob thought he was alone on that hard ground. He thought he was sleeping unprotected. But heaven was open over his head the whole time.

That is what tonight is. You may feel alone in your bed. You may feel defenseless when you close your eyes. But the Lord is in this place. He is in your room. He is over your bed. And he is going to keep you wherever you go, even into your dreams.
