[THE QUIET HOUSE]

And now, let the warfare settle. Let the fire that burned at the walls become the warmth that fills the rooms. The battle at the door is over. The enemy has been turned away. And what remains inside your house tonight is peace.

Breathe slowly. Feel the stillness coming into the room. This is what a covered house feels like. Quiet. Safe. Held.

Isaiah 32 and verse 18 says, And my people shall dwell in a peaceable habitation, and in sure dwellings, and in quiet resting places. A peaceable habitation. A sure dwelling. A quiet resting place. That is your home tonight. Not a battlefield. A resting place.

Let the peace of God settle over the living room. Over the kitchen. Over the hallway. Over every bedroom where someone you love is sleeping. Let it settle over your own bed. Let it rest on your pillow. Let it come down over your mind like a soft blanket, quieting every thought.

You do not have to stay awake to guard your house. You do not have to listen for every sound in the night. You do not have to keep the watch. The one who keeps your house neither slumbers nor sleeps. He has the watch tonight. So you can rest.

Psalm 4 and verse 8 says, I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, Lord, only makest me dwell in safety. Only the Lord. Not the locks. Not the alarm. Not your own strength. The Lord makes you dwell in safety. And he is doing it right now.

Let your shoulders loosen. Let your breathing slow. Let the heaviness you carried into this night lift off you and stay outside the door, where the blood has stopped everything else. You are home. You are covered. You are safe.

[THE SEAL]

Now speak these last words softly over your house, and let them be the final thing that fills the room before you sleep. My house is covered by the blood of Jesus. My doors are sealed. My windows are covered. My walls are fire. My foundation is the Rock. My family is kept. Angels stand guard around me. I lie down in peace, and I sleep, for the Lord alone makes me dwell in safety.

Father, we seal every word spoken tonight over this home in the precious blood of Jesus Christ. We seal it in fire. We seal it in peace. Let this house be a sanctuary from this night forward. Let it be a place of rest, of love, of your presence, and of praise. And let every person under this roof wake in the morning covered, refreshed, and strong.

In the mighty name of Jesus Christ, the Lamb whose blood is on our door. Amen.
