# Video Prompt: "Every Evil Arrow Returns to Sender Tonight"

Use: image-to-video. Upload the generated master image as the start frame (and the end frame too, if the tool allows it, for a seamless loop). Generate one 8–10 second clip and loop it for the full video.

## MASTER VIDEO PROMPT (for the master image)

**Video Prompt:**
Gentle, almost still cinematic motion on a sacred night scene. The engraved cross on the bronze shield glows with a slow, steady breathing pulse of molten gold light, brightening and dimming over several seconds, and the warm glow ripples softly across the hammered metal. The broken arrows at the foot of the shield smolder, with thin threads of ember-orange fire flickering along the splintered shafts and wisps of smoke rising slowly from them. The candle flame to the left flickers softly and sways. Crimson smoke drifts slowly from left to right through the stone chamber behind the shield. In the dark upper background, faint streaks of fire glide slowly away into the distance and fade. Glowing embers and ash particles float upward through the air at a slow, calm pace. The camera holds almost still with an extremely slow push-in toward the shield. No people, no sudden movement, no new arrows striking, no cuts. Reverent, protective, triumphant atmosphere. Painterly devotional art style preserved exactly; crimson, ember orange and molten gold palette; no cool tones.

- **Camera Motion:** Locked off with a very slow push-in of about 2 percent over the clip
- **Motion Elements:** A pulsing glow on the cross, smoldering fire on the broken arrows, rising smoke, candle flicker, drifting crimson smoke, slow fire streaks fading in the background, rising embers
- **Speed:** Slow and calm, suitable for sleep listening
- **Duration:** 8–10 seconds, looped

**Negative Prompt:**
fast motion, arrows flying toward the camera, impacts, explosions, camera shake, zoom bursts, people, faces, hands, morphing objects, melting shapes, shield bending, flicker artifacts, strobing, color shift, blue tones, text, logos, watermark, low quality, distortion

**Settings (most image-to-video tools):**
- Aspect ratio 16:9, 1080p or higher
- Motion strength: low (about 2–3 out of 10)
- Creativity or prompt strength: low to keep the painting unchanged
- Use the same image as the start and end frame for a seamless loop

The translucent dome of crimson-gold light over the hill shimmers softly, with faint ripples where it meets the sky. A few distant fire streaks drift slowly upward and away into the clouds and fade, at an unhurried pace. Crimson storm clouds roll very slowly overhead. The house windows glow with a soft, steady warmth. Embers and ash rise slowly through the air. Camera almost still with a very slow push-in toward the cross. Calm, protective, majestic mood; painterly devotional style and crimson-gold palette preserved; no cool tones.

## VIDEO PROMPT: ALTERNATE B (the sealed letter)

Gentle, almost still motion on a candlelit table. The thin line of ember fire along the parchment edges glows and creeps very slowly, never consuming the letter. The crimson wax seal catches the light with a soft pulse. The candle flame flickers and sways. Thin smoke rises from the parchment edges and curls slowly upward through the shaft of golden light. Embers float upward. Camera locked with a very slow push-in. Quiet, solemn, decisive mood; painterly style and crimson-gold palette preserved; no cool tones.

Negative prompt: letter burning away, paper unfolding, writing appearing, fast motion, camera shake, hands, people, text, logos, morphing, blue tones.

## LOOP AND EDIT ASSEMBLY

1. Generate the 8–10 second clip from the master image and check that the first and last frames match.
2. If the loop shows a jump, add a 1-second crossfade at the loop point, or play the clip forward and then reversed.
3. Repeat the clip to cover the full voiceover (about 48 minutes for one cycle, about 2.5 hours for three).
4. Add a slow zoom of 5–8 percent across each prayer cycle in the editor, on top of the clip's own motion.
5. Add the ember and ash overlay and the smoke overlay at low opacity.
6. Burn in the captions: bold white sans-serif, soft shadow, centered at the bottom, 1 to 2 lines.
