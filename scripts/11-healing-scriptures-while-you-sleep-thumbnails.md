# Thumbnails: "Healing Scriptures While You Sleep"

How to build each one: generate the background art from the prompt (the left side is left dark and empty), then add the text in Canva or Photoshop using the text spec below. If you use a generator that renders text well (Ideogram, GPT image), use the "All-in-one" line instead.

## Text spec (new short style, all 5)
- **Words:** 2–5 words in total, on 2 lines. Nothing else on the image except an optional small channel ribbon.
- **Size:** the two lines together fill the left half of the frame. Check that they read clearly when the thumbnail is shrunk to phone size.
- **Font:** Anton or Oswald Bold, all caps, tight leading.
- **Line 1:** silver-white metallic with a 6–8 px dark stroke and a soft black outer glow.
- **Line 2:** warm gold gradient (#FFF1C1 at the top, then #F7C64A, then #E8892B at the bottom) with the same stroke and glow. Softer than the fire-gold used on the warfare videos.
- **Ribbon (optional, small):** SHIELDED TONIGHT PRAYERS in white small caps under the headline.
- **Layout:** text on the left 55%, the glowing subject on the right 45%, bottom-right corner clear for the timestamp. Canvas is 1280×720.
