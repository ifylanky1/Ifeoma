# Video Prompt: "Healing Scriptures While You Sleep"

Use: image-to-video. Upload the generated master image as the start frame (and the end frame too, if the tool allows it, for a seamless loop). Generate one 8–10 second clip and loop it for the full video.

## MASTER VIDEO PROMPT (for the master image)

**Video Prompt:**
Very gentle, almost still cinematic motion on a peaceful night scene. The pages of the open Bible glow with a slow, soft breathing pulse of warm golden light. Tiny glowing motes of light rise slowly from the pages and drift upward like sleepy fireflies, fading as they rise. The candle flame sways softly and steadily. A thin wisp of candle smoke curls slowly upward. Through the window, the warm glow on the horizon shimmers faintly. The camera holds almost still with an extremely slow push-in toward the Bible. The Bible pages do not turn. No people, no sudden movement, no cuts. Peaceful, tender, restful atmosphere. Painterly devotional art style preserved exactly; warm amber, honey gold and soft crimson palette; no cool tones.

- **Camera Motion:** Locked off with a very slow push-in of about 1–2 percent over the clip
- **Motion Elements:** A soft pulsing glow from the pages, rising motes of light, a gently swaying candle flame, curling smoke, a faint shimmer on the horizon
- **Speed:** Very slow and calm, suitable for falling asleep
- **Duration:** 8–10 seconds, looped

**Negative Prompt:**
pages turning, wind, fire spreading, sparks, fast motion, camera shake, zoom bursts, people, faces, hands, morphing objects, melting shapes, flicker artifacts, strobing, color shift, blue tones, text, letters, logos, watermark, low quality, distortion

**Settings (most image-to-video tools):**
- Aspect ratio 16:9, 1080p or higher
- Motion strength: very low (about 1–2 out of 10)
- Creativity or prompt strength: low to keep the painting unchanged
- Use the same image as the start and end frame for a seamless loop

## VIDEO PROMPT: ALTERNATE A (the river of life)

Very gentle motion in a quiet valley at night. The golden river flows slowly and smoothly. A few glowing leaves drift down from the tree and float slowly on the water. The tree's leaves shimmer faintly. Motes of light rise slowly. The horizon glow pulses softly. Camera almost still with a very slow push-in toward the tree. Healing, serene mood; painterly style and warm gold palette preserved; no cool tones.

Negative prompt: rushing water, wind, branches swaying hard, fast motion, camera shake, people, animals, text, logos, morphing, blue tones.

## VIDEO PROMPT: ALTERNATE B (the quiet bedroom)

Very gentle motion in a calm bedroom at night. The candle flame on the nightstand sways softly. The cross above the bed glows with a slow, faint pulse. Soft amber light from the window shifts very slightly. Tiny motes of light drift through the air. Camera locked with a very slow push-in toward the bed. Safe, calm, restful mood; painterly style and warm palette preserved; no cool tones.

Negative prompt: people, figures in the bed, shadows moving, door opening, curtains blowing, fast motion, camera shake, text, logos, morphing, blue tones.

## LOOP AND EDIT ASSEMBLY

1. Generate the 8–10 second clip from the master image and check that the first and last frames match.
2. If the loop shows a jump, add a 1-second crossfade at the loop point, or play the clip forward and then reversed.
3. Repeat the clip to cover the full voiceover (about an hour).
4. Group all the copies into one compound clip, then add one very slow zoom of 3–5 percent across the whole thing.
5. Add a soft golden motes overlay at low opacity.
6. Export from the CapCut desktop app.
