## 2. AI video prompts (copy-paste, one per clip)

AI video tools make clips of about 5–10 seconds, so generate each scene separately, then join them in CapCut.
Add this **style line** to the end of every prompt so the clips match:

> *Cinematic, luxury fragrance commercial, vertical 9:16, shallow depth of field, warm golden colour grade, soft glowing skin, 4K, slow elegant camera movement, no text, no logos.*

**Clip 1 (Hook):**

**Clip 5 (Application):**
> A woman applies perfume oil with a rollerball to her neck and behind her ear, eyes closed, calm and confident smile, soft window light, satin robe, elegant slow motion.

| Tool | Best for | Notes |

| **Luma Dream Machine** | Smooth luxury product orbits | Free tier |
| **OpenAI Sora** | Lifestyle scenes | Paid ChatGPT plans |
| **CapCut** (free) | **Join all clips, add text, music, transitions, auto-captions** | Has 9:16 templates; export 1080p |
| **Canva** | Quick alternative: animate your product photos + text without AI video | Easiest option if you're short on time |

### Simple workflow
1. Generate Clips 1–9 in **Veo or Kling** (paste each prompt + the style line).
2. Turn your **real bottle photos** into moving shots with **Kling/Luma image-to-video**.
3. Open **CapCut**, set the canvas to 9:16, line the clips up in the script order.
4. Add the on-screen text (bold, clean font, white or gold), trending audio, and auto-captions.
5. Keep it **under 30 seconds**, and make sure the hook text shows in the **first second**.
6. Post between 6–9pm, pin the "Which one are you?" comment, and reply to every DM fast.

**No-AI option:** film it yourself on your phone. Shoot an oil drop on your wrist in sunlight, then each bottle on marble/black stone/silk, and use the same script and text. Real footage of your real product often converts best.

## 5. Important: the designer-inspired oils

You're right not to use the designer names on your branding. Keep the same rule in the ad:
- **Don't** say or show "Amouage", "Love Tuberose", "Guidance", "Amyris" or any brand logos/bottles in the video, caption or hashtags.
- **Do** call them your **"luxury-inspired collection"** or give them your own VPHY names, and lead the ad with your **signature blends** (Lumina, Amber 27, Noir), since those are fully yours.
- Customers who want a specific scent can DM you, and you can talk to them privately about scent profiles (e.g. "rich tuberose floral", "creamy iris and amber").

Using designer brand names in public ads can get posts taken down or bring trademark complaints, so this keeps your page safe.
