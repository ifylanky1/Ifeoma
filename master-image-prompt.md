# Master Image Prompt (Dark Archive Macro)

Paste this straight into your image generator. Replace only the [BRACKETS]; keep everything else word for word so every image matches.

---

## The prompt

[SUBJECT: the object, its era, material and condition], [ENVIRONMENT: where it sits], [ACTION: what is happening in this frozen moment]. Camera: [CAMERA]. Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Mood: [MOOD]. Photoreal 3D CGI render, museum-artifact still life, low-key lighting with steep falloff into near-black, desaturated palette of charcoal, graphite, worn brown wood, aged steel and dull bronze with warm brass accents, slight cool green-grey in the shadows, faint atmospheric haze, crisp specular rim highlights on metal edges, extremely high surface detail with scratches, tool marks, oxidation and wood grain, shallow depth of field with soft bokeh, subject off-center with deep dark negative space, no people, no faces, no hands, no text, no watermark, 16:9.

**Negative prompt** (if your tool has one): people, faces, hands, text, letters, logo, watermark, cartoon, illustration, painting, anime, oversaturated colors, bright background, flat lighting, plastic look, low detail, blurry subject, deformed object, duplicate objects

---

## Quick-pick options

**[ENVIRONMENT]**
- on a dark worn wooden table
- on a black museum plinth
- inside a dim [era] workshop with glowing forge coals
- in an empty [location] at dusk, drifting smoke
- floating in darkness as an exploded view of its parts
- shown as a cutaway cross-section on a dark table

**[CAMERA]**
- macro close-up, razor-thin depth of field
- high 3/4 angle tabletop close-up
- low side angle along the object, deep perspective
- eye-level frontal close-up
- top-down overhead shot
- first-person view looking out through [visor / eye slit]
- low-angle wide establishing shot

**[LIGHTING]**
- single soft key light from upper left, steep falloff into near-black, crisp rim highlights
- low raking side light from the right, long soft shadows, glints along edges
- soft backlight creating a glowing rim silhouette, front in shadow
- narrow overhead spotlight forming a pool of light, everything else in black
- warm flickering forge or flame glow mixed with cold grey fill

**[MOOD]**
ancient and reverent · tense and technical · ominous · triumphant · grim aftermath · clever and satisfying · melancholic · epic scale

**To show people without showing people:** put the object on "a featureless matte-grey display mannequin head", use a first-person view, or show an empty place where people would be.
